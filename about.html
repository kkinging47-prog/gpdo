About GPDO

A development organization built around human dignity.

We exist to help underserved communities gain the opportunity, capacity and resilience needed to shape a better future.

Our story

We believe lasting development is created with communities, not merely for them.

Global Passion Development Organization (GPDO) is a non-profit organization committed to empowering marginalized communities through integrated and sustainable development initiatives. Established under the Companies Act, 2019 (Act 992) as a Company Limited by Guarantee, GPDO exists to serve humanity with integrity, innovation and impact.

Founded in 2024 by Bishop Dr. Akoto and Rev. Mrs. Akoto, the organization was created to help bridge persistent gaps in education, health, opportunity, social inclusion and climate resilience while complementing existing public and private efforts.

Passion + partnershipOur role is to connect local insight with practical resources, capable partners and accountable delivery.
Our vision

A world where marginalized communities thrive through inclusive education, gender equality, quality healthcare, climate resilience and social justice.

Our mission

To empower underserved communities by implementing integrated programs in education, healthcare, gender equity and climate action, while complementing government efforts and fostering innovative solutions for sustainable development and human dignity.

Our approach

Development should be participatory, inclusive and sustainable.

We combine community engagement with responsible governance and partnerships that can translate ideas into durable outcomes.

Community-led

We work closely with local communities to understand real needs before designing solutions.

Outcome-oriented

We seek measurable, practical improvements rather than activity for activity's sake.

Accountable

Transparency, ethical governance and responsible stewardship are core to how we intend to work.

Collaborative

We partner with government, civil society, communities and private institutions around shared goals.
